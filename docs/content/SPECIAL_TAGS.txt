Structure Tags

[Intro]	Instrumental opening	First 8-16 bars, sets the tone
[Verse]	Main narrative sections	Storytelling parts of your song
[Pre-Chorus]	Build-up section	Creates tension before chorus
[Chorus]	Main hook	Catchy, repeated section
[Post-Chorus]	Hook extension	Maintains energy after chorus
[Bridge]	Contrasting section	Mid-song variation, often key change
[Outro]	Closing section	Wind down and ending
[Hook]	Catchy melodic phrase	Memorable instrumental or vocal line
